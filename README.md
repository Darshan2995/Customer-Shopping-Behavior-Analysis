# 🛍️ Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

End-to-End Data Analytics project that analyzes customer shopping behavior using Python, SQL, PostgreSQL, and Power BI. The project uncovers purchasing patterns, customer segments, subscription trends, product performance, and revenue drivers to support data-driven business decision-making.

## 🚀 Features

✅ Data Cleaning & Preprocessing

✅ Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

✅ Customer Segmentation (New, Returning, Loyal)

✅ Revenue Analysis by Gender & Age Group

✅ Subscription Behavior Analysis

✅ Product Performance Analysis

✅ Discount & Promotion Insights

✅ SQL-Based Business Analytics

✅ Interactive Power BI Dashboard

✅ Business Recommendations

## 📊 Core Analysis Modules

### Customer Segmentation

Classifies customers into New, Returning, and Loyal segments based on purchase history.

### Revenue Analysis

Analyzes revenue contribution across gender, age groups, product categories, and subscription status.

### Subscription Insights

Evaluates spending patterns and revenue contribution of subscribers versus non-subscribers.

### Product Performance

Identifies top-rated products, most purchased products, and category-wise sales performance.

### Discount Analysis

Measures the impact of discounts and promotions on customer purchasing behavior.

### Dashboard Analytics

Interactive Power BI dashboard with KPIs, filters, revenue trends, customer segments, and category performance.

## 📈 Key Results

* Analyzed 3,900 customer transactions across 18 attributes.
* Identified Loyal Customers as the largest customer segment.
* Found non-subscribers contributed significantly higher overall revenue.
* Discovered male customers generated nearly 2x more revenue than female customers.
* Built an interactive Power BI dashboard for real-time business insights.
